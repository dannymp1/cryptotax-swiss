Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 March 2026 · CryptoDeclare · Geneva, Switzerland

1. Who we are

CryptoDeclare is operated by Daniel Michaud-Payet, a sole trader based in Geneva, Switzerland. Contact: hello@cryptodeclare.ch.

2. What data we collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our service:

  • Transaction files you upload (CSV/Excel) — processed in-session in your browser and never stored on our servers after your report is generated.
  • Email address — when you contact us via the contact form or purchase a paid plan.
  • Payment data — processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

3. How we use your data

  • To generate your Swiss crypto tax report.
  • To respond to your support enquiries.
  • To process payments via Stripe.

We do not sell, share or rent your data to any third party. We do not use your data for marketing without explicit consent.

4. Data processing location

Your transaction data is processed entirely client-side in your browser using JavaScript. No transaction data is transmitted to or stored on our servers. Your email address, if provided, is stored securely within our contact and payment processors (Formspree and Stripe), both of which are GDPR-compliant.

5. Cookies

CryptoDeclare does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics on this site. The only external services loaded are Google Fonts (for typography) and Stripe (for payments).

6. Your rights under nDSG / GDPR

As a Swiss resident or EU/EEA resident, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to processing of your data.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@cryptodeclare.ch.

7. Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months. Payment records are retained as required by Swiss accounting law (10 years). Transaction files you upload are never stored beyond your browser session.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@cryptodeclare.ch.
