Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 April 2026 · CryptoDeclare · Geneva, Switzerland

1. Who we are

CryptoDeclare is operated by Daniel Michaud-Payet, a sole trader based in Nyon, Switzerland. Contact: hello@cryptodeclare.ch.

Data Controller: The data controller responsible for processing your personal data is Daniel Michaud-Payet, Route de Saint-Cergue 24Bis, Unit 20, 1260 Nyon, Switzerland.

2. What data we collect

We collect only the minimum data necessary to provide our service:

  • Transaction files you upload (CSV/Excel) — processed in-session in your browser. We do not store transaction data on our servers after your report is generated.
  • Email address — when you contact us via the contact form or purchase a paid plan.
  • Payment data — processed entirely by Stripe. We never see or store your card details.

3. How we use your data

  • To generate your Swiss crypto tax report.
  • To respond to your support enquiries.
  • To process payments via Stripe.

We do not sell, share or rent your data to any third party. We do not use your data for marketing without explicit consent.

4. Data processing location

Your transaction data is processed entirely client-side in your browser using JavaScript. No transaction data is transmitted to or stored on our servers. Your email address, if provided, is stored securely within our third-party processors.

We use the following third-party processors:

  • Stripe — payment processing (stripe.com)
  • Resend — transactional email delivery (resend.com)
  • Formspree — contact form handling (formspree.io)

These providers process personal data on our behalf in accordance with their own privacy policies. Some of these providers may process data outside Switzerland or the EU. In such cases, appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses are in place.

5. Cookies

CryptoDeclare does not use tracking cookies or advertising cookies. We do not use Google Analytics or any third-party analytics on this site. Google Fonts are loaded from Google servers, which may result in the transmission of your IP address to Google. Stripe is loaded for payment processing.

5a. Legal basis for processing

We process your personal data based on the following legal grounds:

  • Performance of a contract — to provide the crypto tax report and process your payment.
  • Legitimate interest — to respond to your enquiries and operate the service.
  • Legal obligation — for accounting and record-keeping as required by Swiss law.

6. Your rights under nDSG / GDPR

As a Swiss resident or EU/EEA resident, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you.
  • Request correction or deletion of your data.
  • Object to processing of your data.
  • Lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (FDPIC).

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at hello@cryptodeclare.ch.

7. Data retention

Contact form submissions are retained for up to 12 months. Payment records are retained as required by Swiss accounting law (10 years). Transaction files you upload are never stored beyond your browser session.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top of this page reflects the latest revision. Continued use of the service constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at hello@cryptodeclare.ch.
