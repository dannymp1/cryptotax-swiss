01 — Process

Three steps to a
clean declaration

01

Import transactions

Upload a CSV from supported exchanges such as Kraken, Binance, Coinbase, Swissquote, and SwissBorg — or use our structured CSV import for other exports.

02

We calculate

Our engine applies FIFO, converts fiat values to CHF using official ESTV / BAZG monthly average rates, values 31.12 holdings using ICTax / ESTV year-end token values where available, and identifies reportable income events.

03

Download your report

Receive a downloadable report formatted for Swiss tax reporting. Review it and attach it as supporting documentation to your annual declaration.

06 — FAQ

Common
questions

Yes. Capital gains on crypto are generally tax-free for private individuals in Switzerland — but the holdings must be declared as assets, and income from staking, mining, or professional trading is taxable. We calculate all of this for you.
Our report is designed around federal ESTV guidance, which is commonly used across Swiss cantons. We include guidance notes for Zurich, Geneva, Zug, Vaud, and Bern where local variations are commonly raised.
Where your export already includes CHF values, we use them directly. Otherwise USD, EUR, GBP, and USD-linked amounts are converted with official ESTV / BAZG monthly average rates, and year-end wealth uses ICTax / ESTV published 31.12 token values where available.
We process transactions in-session and do not persist your portfolio data to our servers after your report is generated. We are incorporated in Switzerland and subject to Swiss data protection law (nDSG).
You can upload full transaction history from prior years. Each generated report is created for the tax year covered by the uploaded data, which is useful when preparing past declarations one year at a time.
Staking rewards and similar income events identified in supported CSV exports are reported separately from capital gains. For complex DeFi activity, professional trading, or edge cases, we recommend review by a Swiss tax advisor.
You can use our structured CSV import — files with date, asset, quantity, type, and price columns are parsed automatically. If your exchange export is unusual, contact us and we can help you map it.
